Curiosity is the seed from which understanding grows. To learn is to remain
willing to be wrong, and to be wrong is to discover where the truth was hiding.

A good question is worth more than a confident answer. The mind that listens
carefully often finds what the mind that speaks first overlooks.

Patience is a kind of attention. The world reveals itself slowly to those who
are willing to wait without filling the silence.

Every problem has a shape. Once you can describe the shape clearly, the
solution is usually already implied. Most confusion is just a description that
hasn't been written down yet.

Kindness costs little and changes much. A small word at the right time can
unbend a whole afternoon.

Code, like prose, is read more than it is written. Write it for the person
who comes after you, even if that person is yourself in six months.

The night is quiet, the stars are old, and the mind that wonders about them
is younger than any question it can ask. To stand under the open sky is to
remember that you are part of something larger than your worries.

Practice is not repetition; it is repetition with attention. The third time
you do a thing carefully is worth more than the hundredth time you do it
mindlessly.

A library is a long conversation between people who have never met. To open a
book is to take a seat at that table.

Hope is not optimism. Optimism expects things to go well; hope keeps working
even when they do not. The first is a forecast, the second is a discipline.

Simplicity is hard. Anyone can make a thing complicated; making it simple
again is the real craft. Cut what is decorative, keep what carries weight.

The mountain does not care whether you climb it. That is what makes climbing
it worthwhile. Meaning is something you bring with you, not something you
find at the summit.
